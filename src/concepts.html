Concept Studies

Everything begins with Concept.

Each Concept is studied on its own. Choose the ones most relevant to your life now.

Concepts are the ideas and meanings we accept as true about ourselves, other people, relationships, the Divine Source, and life. They begin forming through early experiences, observation, teaching, interpretation, and the conclusions we draw from what happens to us.

Concepts organise experience. They influence what we notice, how we interpret circumstances, what we expect, and how we respond. When a Concept is repeatedly reinforced, it can become a belief and give direction to Patterns of Behavior.

By becoming aware of the Concepts operating within us, we can understand why certain experiences and Patterns continue to repeat. When the underlying Concept changes, new choices, responses, and possibilities become available.
