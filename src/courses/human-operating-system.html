01

What this is

Every other study at the Institute describes one part of the machinery. This one describes the machinery. It is the account of how a Concept becomes a belief, how a belief becomes an expectation, how an expectation produces a Pattern, and how the result is read back as evidence that the Concept was true all along.

That circuit is why life repeats. Seeing it is not the same as being free of it, but nothing is possible before it is seen.

Which edition

The introductory presentation is the shorter route through the same territory, and it is the one to take if you want the shape of the system before committing to the detail. The complete written study is the reference — the edition you return to while working through the Concepts and the Schools.
