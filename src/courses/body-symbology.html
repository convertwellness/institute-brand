01

What this is

The symbolic meaning of the body — of its systems, of the Five Senses, of symptoms and of accidents. Not a diagnosis and not a substitute for one. A symptom is read here as a message rather than a verdict, and the study is in learning to read.

03

Which depth

Three Tiers, each a book in its own right. They are the same subject at three distances, and they are meant to be taken in order — the first ends by pointing at the second.

  1. 1 Symbolic Body Atlas A Guide to Understanding the Messages of the Body. Fifty-six pages. Builds awareness: why the body communicates, symptoms as signals rather than problems, the Four Selves, and a quick-reference map of fifteen body regions from the head to the feet. Ends with three awareness tools — a journal, a ten-region observation worksheet, and a “where do you hold stress?” body map.
  2. 2 The Symbolic Body Atlas — Tier 2 From Signals to Meaning. One hundred and eighty-seven pages, moving from symptom to Pattern identification. Where the first book teaches you to notice, this one teaches you to read.
  3. 3 The Mastery Atlas — Tier 3 The Complete System of the Symbolic Body, Patterns, and Human Transformation. Three hundred and eighty-six pages. The reference to own if you expect to look things up for years.
