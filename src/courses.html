Courses

Four Courses at launch.

They are not four of the same thing. One is a ten-week programme, one a presentation or a written study, one an eBook at three depths.

Courses are organised studies that guide students through a specific subject, question, or area of life. Each Course brings together related Institute presentations, Concepts, Patterns, and Universal Principles in a clear and purposeful sequence.

Courses may introduce a subject, develop a deeper understanding, or teach students how to apply what they have learned. They allow students to focus upon a particular need or interest while still understanding how it connects with the greater Institute teaching.

Through each Course, students move from information to awareness, from awareness to understanding, and from understanding to conscious application in daily life.
