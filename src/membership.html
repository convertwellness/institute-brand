Three kinds of access

Streaming

Material may be viewed for as long as the membership is active.

Download

Selected materials may be kept for personal study, within the allowance of the tier.

Ownership

A Course or programme you purchase or complete stays with you permanently.

Downloaded material carries a personal-use licence. It is not permission to reproduce the material or to teach from it.
