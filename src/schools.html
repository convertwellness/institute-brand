The Schools of the Institute

Each School is a twelve-week program.

The Schools provide organised areas of study through which students can explore the major dimensions of human life, consciousness, relationships, and spiritual development. Each School brings together related Concepts, Patterns, Universal Principles, and existing Institute teachings within one coherent field of understanding.

The Schools allow students to enter the Institute through the subjects most relevant to their present needs and interests. Although each School has a particular focus, they remain interconnected because every part of life operates within the same Human Operating System and Divine order.

Through progressive study, students learn to recognise what is operating within them, understand how it developed, and apply Universal Principles to their choices, relationships, challenges, and personal growth. The purpose of every School is to transform knowledge into conscious participation in life.
