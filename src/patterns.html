Patterns of Behavior

Twelve Patterns to begin with.

Patterns of Behavior may influence how we think, choose, relate and respond to life.

The purpose is not to judge or label, but to develop awareness of the Patterns operating beneath familiar reactions. Each may be studied on its own, or alongside the Concept that creates it.
