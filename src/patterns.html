Patterns of Behavior

Twelve Patterns to begin with.

Patterns of Behavior may influence how we think, choose, relate and respond to life.

The purpose is not to judge or label, but to develop awareness of the Patterns operating beneath familiar reactions. Each is studied on its own.

Patterns are repeated ways of thinking, responding, choosing, and behaving that develop from the Concepts and beliefs held within consciousness. They are created to express and validate what a person has accepted as true about themselves, others, relationships, and life.

Patterns are not random, nor are they simply personal flaws. Each Pattern has an underlying purpose and reveals something that consciousness is attempting to protect, prove, avoid, receive, or understand.

By learning to recognise our Patterns, we can look beneath repeated behaviour and discover the Concept that gives it direction. Awareness allows us to understand why the Pattern developed, what it continues to produce, and what must change for a different experience to become possible.
