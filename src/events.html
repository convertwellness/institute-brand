Live Events

Seminars from the Institute.

Sessions on a single subject, taught live. Two are named so far.

Live Events provide opportunities for students and members to experience the Institute’s teachings through direct instruction, conversation, questions, and shared exploration. They bring the material to life by connecting Universal Principles, Concepts, and Patterns with real experiences and present-day concerns.

Live Events may include lectures, workshops, discussions, question-and-answer sessions, special presentations, and community gatherings. Each event allows participants to deepen their understanding, gain practical insight, and learn from the experiences of others.

The purpose of Live Events is to create a living environment in which knowledge becomes personal awareness, shared understanding, and conscious application.
