    The Institute / Membership

    Membership

    You are not being asked to believe anything.

    You are being asked to look at what is already operating, and to decide for yourself whether you recognise it. Members receive preferred pricing on qualifying Courses, books and materials.

    Three memberships

    Entry Institute Member
    To be confirmed a month

    Accessible monthly participation, for continuing exposure to the teachings without committing to a complete programme.

    • Three credits toward purchases
    • One featured presentation each month
    • Selected introductory material
    • Question-and-answer sessions every six weeks
    • The member community
    • Member pricing on books, Courses and Schools
    • Recordings while the membership is active
    • Credit toward Core Studies after a defined period
    Join monthly
    A full year Sustaining Member
    To be confirmed a year

    A full year of guided learning.

    • Everything in Institute Membership
    • The complete archive of membership teachings, after three months
    • Quarterly special presentations and workshops
    • Up to forty-eight downloads a year
    • Early registration for live events
    • A study-path session twice a year
    • Priority questions in selected live sessions
    • A certificate recognising annual participation
    Join for a year
    The complete programme Core Studies Member
    To be confirmed for three years

    An introductory price for the first three years, against a figure for three years thereafter.

    Limited to twelve students

    • Everything in Sustaining Membership
    • Enrolment in the complete Core Studies Programme
    • A guided progression through the essential teachings
    • Personal consultation with an instructor every two months
    • All slide presentations, manuals and study guides
    • Personal Action Plans and reflection materials
    • Permanent access to completed Core Studies material
    • Progress tracking, and a Certificate of Core Studies
    • Eligibility to enter the specialised Schools
    Apply for a place

    Three kinds of access

    Streaming

    Material may be viewed for as long as the membership is active.

    Download

    Selected materials may be kept for personal study, within the allowance of the tier.

    Ownership

    A Course or programme you purchase or complete stays with you permanently.

    Downloaded material carries a personal-use licence. It is not permission to reproduce the material or to teach from it.
