    Join as Counselor

    Annual membership is ten months' price for twelve months. Prices are held until Michael confirms them.

    How material is released

    • Annual members receive their tier's material up front. Monthly members receive it at a steady pace.
    • Explorer and Discoverer, monthly: each School releases one week at a time over twelve weeks, and the next School opens when the first is finished. The eBook unlocks with the second payment. One disease study a quarter.
    • Counselor, monthly: the first month unlocks everything Discoverer receives, the Foundation of the Institute Course and the first live session. Months two to eleven unlock one School a month with its related collections and Family studies. Month twelve unlocks the rest. Every month brings a live session and one disease study.
    • Unreleased items can be bought ahead at the member's own discount. A monthly member can switch to annual at any time by paying the balance, and everything unlocks.
    • Schools unlock in the order the Institute sets.
    • Unused free monthly or quarterly benefits do not carry over.

    Upgrading

    Everything you have already spent counts toward bundles, courses and tier upgrades. An upgrade starts its benefits immediately and the new rate bills on the next cycle.

    What you keep

    Streamed

    Presentations, voiced or self-guided, are streamed for as long as you have access. They cannot be downloaded.

    Downloaded

    Written material can be downloaded, carries your name, and is yours to keep for personal study, even after you cancel.

    One person

    A membership is for one person. Shared accounts can be suspended.

    Open question · O7

    Whether any tier has a limit on downloads.

    Refunds and cancelling

    Pending legal review · O11

    Monthly: a full refund of the first payment if cancelled within 30 days, once per member. After that, cancel any time, effective at the end of the paid month. Annual paid in full: not cancellable or refundable, with full access for twelve months, and a reminder at least fourteen days before it renews. How to cancel, and the contact address, are still blank.
