    Six pieces with nowhere to go yet

    Michael wrote these on 25 September and they are finished. What is missing is not the writing. It is where each one lives. Choose a home for each, add a note if it needs one, then press Copy the answers and paste them back to Sara. Nothing is sent from this page; the answers stay in this browser until you copy them.

    1. 01

      Universal Principles

      Universal Principles are enduring truths that reveal the Divine order operating throughout life.

      Named inside four of the six definitions just written: Schools, Courses, Books and Live Events all say they bring together Universal Principles. Nothing on the site defines it, so this is the one that holds up the others.

    2. 02

      The Concept of Concepts

      Concepts are the foundation upon which life is understood and lived.

      Reads as foundational teaching rather than a Study of one Concept. It may belong with the Human Operating System.

    3. 03

      The Twelve Spiritual Gifts

      The Twelve Spiritual Gifts are Divine qualities placed within every person as potentials to be recognised, developed, and expressed.

      A numbered set, the same shape as the Twelve Patterns, which on this site is either a Course or a School.

    4. 04

      The Twelve Disciplines

      The Twelve Disciplines are conscious practices that help us bring our thoughts, choices, actions, and daily lives into greater harmony with truth and Divine order.

      Written as the companion to the Spiritual Gifts: gifts are what is placed in us, disciplines are the practice. The two probably live together.

    5. 05

      The Six Senses

      The Six Senses are the means through which we receive information, interpret experience, and become aware of both the physical and nonphysical dimensions of life.

      Another numbered set. The Five Senses are already named inside Body Symbology.

    6. 06

      The Four Selves

      The Four Selves are the primary dimensions through which we experience and express human life: the Spiritual Self, Emotional Self, Physical/Material Self, and Social/Intellectual Self.

      The only one of the six already named on the site. It appears in the Human Operating System and in Body Symbology, but it has no page of its own.
