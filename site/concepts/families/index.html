Concept Families

Concepts are rarely met one at a time.

Individual Concepts frequently belong to a larger family of related Concepts.

Concept Family Studies allow students to explore a central area of human experience and recognise the different Concepts and Patterns that may develop within it. Each Family Study may contain approximately ten to twelve significant Patterns.

Four Families open the library, in the order Michael recommends.

Family 01 Acceptance Rejection, Approval, Self-Rejection. Three of the twelve Concepts Family 02 Worth Unworthiness, Inadequacy, Inferiority, Undeserving. Four of the twelve Concepts Family 03 Control Named as one of the four, with no Concepts placed in it yet. Contents awaiting Michael Family 04 Trust Suspicion. One of the twelve Concepts
Awaiting Michael

Three of the twelve Concepts — Abandonment, Shame, and Fear and Doubt — appear in none of the four Family lists. They are shown as unassigned on the Concepts index rather than placed by guess.

His August architecture names fifteen Families, not four: Acceptance, Freedom, Control, Emotional Expression, Belonging, Achievement, Safety and Protection, Responsibility, Relationship, Love, Identity, Worth, Trust, Truth and Communication, and Spiritual Connection. All four launch Families appear in that list, so the four look like a subset rather than a replacement — which would also explain the three unassigned Concepts. Worth confirming with him.
