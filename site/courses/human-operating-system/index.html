    The Human Operating System

    Available now

    Why life repeats, and how consciousness, mind, brain, the Four Selves, Concepts, beliefs, expectations and Patterns work together.

    01

    What this is

    Every other study at the Institute describes one part of the machinery. This one describes the machinery, in the five steps Michael sets out: Concept, Belief System / Pattern, Experience, Fulfilled Expectation, and Validation of Concept.

    That circuit is why life repeats. Seeing it is not the same as being free of it, but nothing is possible before it is seen.

    02

    What it covers

    • Consciousness
    • Mind
    • Brain
    • The Four Selves
    • Concepts
    • Beliefs
    • Expectations
    • Patterns

    03

    Which edition

    The introductory presentation is the shorter route through the same territory, and it is the one to take if you want the shape of the system before committing to the detail. The complete written study is the reference: the edition you return to while working through the Concepts and the Schools.

    04

    Where it sits

    This is not gated. It can be taken before Foundation of the Institute Course, alongside them, or years later, and it neither requires the Foundation nor replaces it.

    It pairs with
    • Foundation of the Institute Course. The Foundation shows you thirty Patterns; this shows you the mechanism producing them.
    • The School of the Human Operating System, which takes the same material further.
    All four Courses at the Institute A free introduction, a ten-week programme, a study in two editions, an eBook at three depths. Further in The Schools of the Institute Six Schools, in the order Michael recommends beginning.
