    Ten-week programme

    Foundation Studies

    Open to begin at any time

    Ten weeks. Each week introduces three Patterns of Behavior that may influence how we think, choose, relate and respond to life.

    01

    What this is

    The purpose is not to judge or label, but to develop awareness of the Patterns operating beneath familiar reactions. A Pattern seen is already a Pattern that has lost some of its authority; that is the whole of the method, and ten weeks is how long it takes to meet thirty of them.

    Foundation Studies is the one course the Institute asks everyone to take before study begins elsewhere. Not because the rest is difficult, but because the rest assumes this vocabulary.
